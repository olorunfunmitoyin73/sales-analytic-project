### Project Overview
---
This data analytics project aims to showcase sales performance of a company producing consumable items for a period of 2 years (2015 & 2016 respectively). By analyzing the fact table after every connections have been created, we seek to display trends through graphical illustrations, showcase performance of each sales persons and also display the KPIs in line with the necessary slicers.

#### Result or Observations
---
Observations from the analyzed data shows that there is no much disparity in the sales performance by product (ranging between 4% to 6%) from the summary table. On the other hand, western region recorded the highest sales with the northeastern region being the least.
However, it is important to note that the report might display a contrary outcome to what was stated above when the information is break down by months or by year with the aid of slicers. Also, other tables incorporated in the presentation to help our analysis and for better understanding.


#### Recommendation
---
For better analyses and presentation of the company's performance, it will be advised that subsequently, the management provides the total cost of production in other to showcase if the company is actually making profit or loss and to equally showcase if the organization was able to achieve its target goals airmark for a given period. 


#### Limitations
---
- It was observed some tables in the data provided appeared more that once. However, this tables were linked to the fact table.
- It was also observed that in the data provided in the fact table, there was no cost of production which made impossible to showcase profit or loss in the KPIs.
